Our Story

The First Scoop

It all started with a small ice cream cart, handcrafted recipes, and a big love for sweet desserts.

2018

We experimented, created, perfected recipes and introduced our signature warm Belgian waffles.

2026

PURE QUALITY GUARANTEE

Crafted Without Shortcuts.

Every batch of gelato and every warm baked waffle is prepared with uncompromising commitment to purity, authentic taste, and zero artificial shortcuts.

100% Pure Veg & Eggless

All ice creams, waffles, crepes, and sundaes are strictly 100% vegetarian & egg-free.

Slow-Churned Micro Batches

Churned in small 5-liter tubs daily to achieve dense, silky creaminess without air fluffing.

Local Dairy & Organic Fruits

Made with pure farm milk, real Madagascar vanilla beans, and sun-ripened fruit pulps.
