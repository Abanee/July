How We Measure Athlete Growth

Progress isn't accidental. We combine clinical testing, video analysis, and continuous feedback to benchmark development.

Milestone 01

Quarterly Skill Matrix

A comprehensive 24-point technical assessment conducted every 90 days covering sport mechanics, ball touch, and form execution.

FrequencyEvery 90 Days
Milestone 02

Biometric & Speed Telemetry

Laser sprint timing gates, vertical leap measurements, and agility cone drills to quantify explosive power and acceleration.

Focus MetricSpeed & Power
Milestone 03

Matchplay Video Breakdown

High-frame-rate recorded match clips reviewed 1-on-1 with coaches to evaluate tactical positioning and decision speed under pressure.

Review FormatCoach 1-on-1
Milestone 04

Parent-Coach Report Cards

Transparent term reports outlining physical improvements, attendance discipline, tactical growth, and personalized next-term targets.

OutputVerified Report Card

Athletic Wellness

Sports Science & Recovery Protocols

Peak performance requires smart recovery. We integrate physio-backed injury prevention and conditioning into every batch.

Functional Movement Screening

Pre-season mobility tests to identify kinetic asymmetries, prevent ligament strain, and prescribe custom pre-hab exercises.

Periodized Load Management

Carefully calculated training volumes that prevent burnout, overtraining, and growth-plate fatigue in developing youth athletes.

Sports Nutrition & Hydration

Nutritional guidelines for game-day carb loading, electrolyte replenishment, and protein intake for optimal muscle recovery.

Active Recovery & Mobility

Post-training foam rolling, dynamic stretching, and breathing protocols to accelerate lactic acid clearance and restore flexibility.
