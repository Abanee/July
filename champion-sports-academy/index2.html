06  ·  Athlete Pathway

From Fundamentals to Competition.

Every athlete enters at the right development stage and progresses with verified coaching milestones.

Ages 5–8 STAGE 01

Foundation & Motor Skills

Agility, hand-eye coordination, balance, listening skills, and building enjoyment through sport.

  • Ratio: 6:1 Athlete to Coach
  • Schedule: 2 sessions / week (60m)
Milestone Focus Movement Competency
Ages 9–12 STAGE 02

Technical Foundations

Position-specific ball mastery, footwork mechanics, reaction speed, and introductory tactical awareness.

  • Ratio: 8:1 Athlete to Coach
  • Schedule: 3 sessions / week (75m)
Milestone Focus Skill Execution
Ages 13–17 STAGE 03

Development & Tactics

Game scenarios, structured strength conditioning, mental pressure handling, and inter-academy leagues.

  • Ratio: 8:1 Athlete to Coach
  • Schedule: 4 sessions / week (90m)
Milestone Focus Competitive Readiness
Ages 18+ STAGE 04

Elite Performance

Video analytics review, individualized conditioning regimens, mental fortitude workshops, and collegiate/pro transition.

  • Ratio: 6:1 / 1:1 Specialization
  • Schedule: 5 sessions / week (120m)
Milestone Focus Pro Scouting
