Our 7-Step Professional Repair Workflow

Every device undergoes a transparent, ESD-safe repair sequence before collection.

STEP 01

Intake Audit

24-point diagnostic test logging screen, cameras, mic, speakers, and battery health.

STEP 02

ESD Disassembly

Thermal pad heating & precision suction opening on grounded anti-static mats.

STEP 03

40x microscope logic board micro-soldering or genuine OEM part installation.

Factory waterproof pressure seal applied under pneumatic clamp press.

STEP 07

Live device functional test with customer & warranty certificate issuance.
