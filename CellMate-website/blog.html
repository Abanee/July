SMARTPHONE HEALTH CHECKLIST

4 Quick Habits to Avoid Costly Repairs

Proactive device maintenance tips curated by CellMate lab technicians.

01

Avoid Thermal Stress

Never leave your smartphone on a car dashboard under direct sunlight. Heat accelerates lithium cell swelling and display glue separation.

02

Use Certified Cables

MFi (Made for iPhone) and USB-IF certified cables regulate current and prevent over-voltage damage to your phone's Tristar/Tigris charging IC.

03

Replace Cracked Glass

Even small hairline glass cracks break the IP68 moisture seal, allowing ambient humidity to seep into the motherboard over time.

04

Calibrate 80% Charge

Enable optimized battery charging on iOS or protect battery mode on Android to prevent holding 100% state-of-charge overnight.
